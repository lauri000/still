# Still 0.1.12

Scroll through multiline text and tap any word to continue in single-line mode.

- Multiline now scrolls normally with your finger, mouse, or trackpad. Press-and-hold and automatic playback are removed from this view.
- Tap a word to open single-line mode paused at that exact word, ready to continue reading.
- Switching modes preserves your place, including after scrolling or rotating the screen. Your saved position also survives closing and reopening the book.
- Single-line hold-to-read, speed controls, and reading options keep their existing behavior.
- Text is laid out in nearby paragraph blocks. The 104,789-word 1984 test book opens successfully with every tested reading-option combination.

Requires Android 9 or newer. Version code: 13. Signed with the existing release key; updating preserves books, positions, and settings.
