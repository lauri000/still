# Still 0.1.11

PDF contents navigation and clearer chapter headings.

- Browse the PDF outline when choosing a starting page or from the reader header.
- Matched headings stay in the scrolling text with larger type and spacing. Single-line reading shows each heading together.

- Multiline keeps its fast nearby-text layout and remains independent of single-line reading options.
