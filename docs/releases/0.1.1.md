# Still 0.1.1

Read with a hold-and-drag gesture in the bottom-right corner.

- Hold the corner to start reading; lift your finger to pause.
- Move right while holding to increase speed, or left to decrease it, relative to your current speed.
- Reading continues smoothly while you adjust speed. The mobile play buttons and speed slider are removed.
- Swipe the surrounding text or tap a word to choose where to resume, independently of the corner gesture.
- Keep your reading position, books, and settings when updating from the signed 0.1.0 release.

Requires Android 9 or newer. This universal APK includes ARM64, ARMv7, x86, and x86_64 libraries.
Version code: 2. Signed with the same Android release key as 0.1.0.

The APK, SHA256SUMS, source commit manifest, and public signing certificate are
attached to this release. Download all four assets into one directory and run
`shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS` to verify their checksums.
