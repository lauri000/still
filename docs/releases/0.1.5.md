# Still 0.1.5

More room for reading, especially in landscape, with simpler Android controls.

- Remove the footer. Page navigation and a thin progress bar now live in the compact header, leaving the rest of the screen for text.
- Hold anywhere in the invisible bottom-right quarter of the reading area to read immediately; release to pause. Move right to increase speed or left to decrease it relative to your current speed.
- Start a swipe outside that corner to scrub backward or forward. Each gesture keeps its role until you lift, even when crossing the boundary. Extra fingers cannot take over.
- Show the floating WPM number only when speed changes, then fade it after adjustments stop.
- Remove tapping words to jump. Keep the page picker and accessible start/pause, speed, and word navigation actions.
- Retain active word size, animated scrolling, focus-letter highlighting, and justified passage rows in both reading views.

This redesign applies to Android. Requires Android 9 or newer. The universal APK
includes ARM64, ARMv7, x86, and x86_64 libraries. Version code: 6.
Signed with the same Android release key as previous releases; updating preserves
your books, reading positions, and settings.

The APK, SHA256SUMS, source commit manifest, and public signing certificate are
attached to this release. Download all four assets into one directory and run
`shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS` to verify their checksums.
